About TECHNOVA

A celebration of technology, creativity and innovation.

TECHNOVA 2026 is a dynamic college fest hosted by RV Institute of Technology and Management, bringing together students, innovators and technology enthusiasts. Experience exciting technical competitions, creative challenges, interactive sessions and networking opportunities. Discover new ideas, showcase your talent and compete with the best minds.

Special Guest

An iconic personality in Indian cinema.

FEATURED GUEST

Rajinikanth

An iconic Indian actor celebrated for his extraordinary screen presence, unique style and decades-long contribution to Indian cinema. His journey represents perseverance, determination and the power of individuality.
